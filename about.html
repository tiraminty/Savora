About Us

Crafting unforgettable Italian dining experiences since 1998.

Our Journey

1998

Savora opened its doors with a dream of bringing authentic Italian cuisine to the community.

2008

Our passion for quality earned us recognition as one of the city's favorite Italian restaurants.

2018

We expanded our dining space while staying true to our traditional family recipes.

Today

Every dish is prepared with fresh ingredients and served with the same passion that started it all.
